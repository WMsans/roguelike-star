# The Parts (Stage 4 of All Parts) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every one of the 169 parts in `docs/design-doc/parts-list.md` exists as a `.tres` def plus one behaviour script, does what its Effect column says, is offered by the shop, and has a test that proves its effect. The 12 old parts retire, and the bodies, the starting kit and the Cook are rebuilt from list parts.

**Architecture:**
- Each part is `game/data/parts/<id>.tres` plus `game/battle/behaviours/parts/<id>.gd` extending `ChBehaviour`. There is one way a part is built: even a one-line stat part has a script.
- Parts change each other through a few hooks on `ChBehaviour`, which `ChBattleContext` asks:
  - `modify_stat`: costs, cooldowns, rates, ranges, decay, cell counts and the like.
  - `modify_gain`: status gains.
  - `modify_hit`: outgoing hits. It exists today.
  - `modify_incoming`: hits being redirected.
  - `prevent_death`
  - `links`: Hub Frame.
- Run-level parts answer `run_event` and `run_stat` through `ChRun`.
- The helpers grow in batch 1 (Tasks 1–4), because the commons need most of them. Later batches add only the hooks their own parts need.
- Batches follow the spec's table. Every task ends with the suite green and its parts in the shop.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4, GDScript, the `noita_engine` PixelWorld, the repo's headless test runner, `tools/balance`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-all-parts-design.md`. The binding parts are:
- Stage 4
- "Decided by the user"
- "Constraints"
- the anti-goals
- "Carried from Stage 1" and "Carried from Stage 2"

Each part's behaviour is its row in `docs/design-doc/parts-list.md`. The status and material rules are in `docs/design-doc/elements-list.md`.

## Global Constraints

- **Tests.**
  - Run one file's tests: `godot --headless --path . -s res://tests/run_tests.gd -- <file-name-filter>`.
  - The whole suite (no filter) must end `N passed, 0 failed` at the end of every task.
  - Run `godot --headless --path . --import` once first, and again after adding a `.tres` or a `class_name`.
  - Smoke: `bash tools/balance/run.sh --suite smoke` must finish ok.
  - Known noise to ignore: engine RID-leak warnings at exit, `ERROR: Condition "!props.has(pinfo.name)"` on import, and the wall-clock flake `test_tower_drives_upright_on_slow_frames`.
- **Determinism.** Fights stay deterministic for a seed. Any random draw uses `ctx.bus.rng`, or an RNG seeded from it.
- **Same rules both sides.** Every part works the same on the enemy's mirrored chariot. Scopes read `part.facing()`, never the side.
- **One damage path.** Every weapon hit goes through `ctx.deal_hit`. Every HP loss goes through `ctx.lose_hp` (Task 3). Every announcement goes through `ctx.bus`.
- **Statuses from the sim** (parts-list §1.3).
  - Parts give Flame, Freeze, Acid, Wet, Oil and Shock only by spawning cells (`ctx.spawn`) or sparks (`ctx.throw_sparks`).
  - Only Block, Speedup and Slow go on directly, through `ctx.give_status`.
- **Ids and files.**
  - `id` is the snake_case of the Name column (`rocket_pod`, `heat_shield`, `trade_in_coupon`, `collectors_shelf`).
  - Files are `game/data/parts/<id>.tres` and `game/battle/behaviours/parts/<id>.gd`, with no `class_name`.
- **Def fields from the row** (spec table; `tests/data/test_parts_list.gd` enforces it from Task 1 on):
  - `rarity` comes from the ID prefix: C common, U uncommon, R rare.
  - `tags` is the Tags column in lowercase.
  - `En` sets `energy_cost`, or `energy_per_second` when the cell is `N/s`.
  - `CD` sets the mode:
    - a number gives `mode = cooldown` with that `cooldown`
    - `Melee` gives `mode = melee`
    - `Beam` gives `mode = beam`
    - `—` gives `mode = wheel` if tagged `wheel`, else `passive`
  - `Int` sets `integrity`.
  - `Rng` sets `proximity_range`: S 12, M 20, L 32, `—` 0.
  - `Ext` sets `extension_slots.size()`.
- **Descriptions are the Effect text.**
  - `description` is one entry: the Effect cell, marked up with `ChKeywords` tags (`{speedup}`, `{flame:Flame}`).
  - With the markup stripped (`{k:shown}` → shown, `{k}` → `ChKeywords.name_of(k)`), it equals the cell character for character.
  - When the builder picks a number the cell leaves open (an explosion's damage, a reach), it writes the number into the parts-list row and the description together.
- **Damage numbers live where tests can read them.** The Effect's main number is `def.damage`. Other numbers are `const`s at the top of the part's script, named after the Effect wording (`BLOCK := 40`). Each part's test asserts the Effect's number literally.
- **Wording rules** (my calls, used by every batch):
  - **Weapon effects with a geometry word** — "Fire", "Launch", "Lob", "Throw", "Spray", "Stab", "jet", "beam", "per tick" — use a projectile, a lob, a reach box or a beam box. A hit lands on the nearest enemy body (a part, or the hull), as `ChBehaviour._nearest_enemy_body` does.
  - **"Deal N damage" with no geometry word** goes straight to the enemy hull: `ctx.deal_hit(part, enemy.chassis, n)`, through `_hit_enemy_body`. This covers C10, C33, U25, R03 and R04.
  - **"Gain N Block"** goes on this part. **"Your body gains"** goes on the chariot. **"Neighbours"** are `_neighbours`, the own parts within `ctx.range_of(part)`.
  - **"For the battle"** is `ChPartRuntime.bonus_damage`, or a field on the behaviour. **"Permanently"** is the instance's run state (Task 23).
  - **"Every N seconds"** clocks count fight seconds (`delta`), not scaled by Speedup or Slow and not paused by Freeze. Only cooldowns and continuous ticks scale.
  - **Melee reach** is `MELEE_REACH = 24` cells (Stage 3's settled number). C05 Stinger's is 48.
- **Stat names are StringName constants** in `ChBattleContext.STAT_*`. Each task adds only the ones it reads.
- **No engine C++** unless a helper's test shows GDScript per-cell work is too slow (more than 2 ms a tick in the smoke run). Note it in the task's commit if it happens.

## Review Focus

1. **The enemy uses every part.** Every catalog part mounted on the mirrored side-1 chariot fires (or holds) against a side-0 rig without an error. Nothing it does lands a HIT on its own side. Task 7's sweep test, which re-runs in every later batch because it walks the catalog.
2. **The target is gone.** A part firing, holding or triggering after the enemy's chassis is freed or every enemy part is broken must not error. Projectiles in flight, lobbed charges and mines with no target simply expire. Task 7's sweep runs a second pass with the enemy killed at 1 s.
3. **A broken part stops, what it made does not.**
   - A broken part's hooks stop at once: `modify_stat`, `modify_gain`, `modify_hit` and `modify_incoming` skip broken parts, as `deal_hit` already does.
   - Cells, crates, mines and lit fuses it already made stay in the world.
   - Task 2 tests the stat hook. Task 6 tests that a Dynamite fuse outlives its thrower.
4. **Loops under the bus cap** (parts-list §6, plus two this plan adds: Shield Battery feeding itself, and Retribution ping-pong). Each loop gets a test in its owning task: 10 s of fight with no hang, and `ctx.time` reaches 10.
5. **The data drifts from the list.** A def's numbers, mode, tags or description disagree with its parts-list row. Task 1's parse test checks every def on disk against its row, and Task 24 also requires all 169 to exist.

### Task 2: Effective stats and scopes

**Files:**
- Modify: `game/battle/behaviours/ch_behaviour.gd`, `game/battle/ch_battle_context.gd`, `game/battle/ch_part_runtime.gd`, `game/battle/ch_chariot_runtime.gd`, `game/battle/ch_event_bus.gd`, `game/battle/ch_enemy_ai.gd`, `game/battle/ch_event.gd`, `game/battle/ch_battle.gd`
- Test: new `tests/battle/test_stats_scopes.gd`, plus updates to `test_continuous.gd` and `test_enemy_ai.gd`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - **`ChBehaviour.modify_stat(ctx, target: Object, stat: StringName, value: float) -> float`.** The base returns `value`.
    - `ChBattleContext.stat(target: Object, stat: StringName, base: float) -> float` folds `base` through the `modify_stat` of every unbroken, non-benched part on both chariots, in side order then mount order.
    - `ctx` keeps the behaviours whose own script declares `modify_stat` (read once in `setup_all`) and asks only those.
  - **`ChBattleContext.setup_all()`** calls every part's `setup`. It replaces `ChBattle`'s own loop at `ch_battle.gd:205` and is the one place Mimic and the bench hook in later (Tasks 23 and 24).
  - **Per-tick refresh.** `ChPartRuntime` gains three fields: `cost: int`, `rate: float` and `cooldown: float`. The builder seeds them from the def. At the start of `_tick_chariot`, `ctx` refreshes them:
    - `cost = roundi(stat(part, STAT_ENERGY_COST, def.energy_cost))`
    - `rate = stat(part, STAT_ENERGY_PER_SECOND, def.energy_per_second)`
    - `cooldown = stat(part, STAT_COOLDOWN, def.cooldown)`
  - **AI reach from the part's edge** (a Stage 3 review nit, fixed here because Stage 4's reaches vary from 4 to 120). `ChEnemyAI.should_press` and `should_hold` measure `dx` from `anchor + facing × half the part's length` and count only targets on the facing side, as `_reach_box` does.
  - **The refreshed fields are read everywhere a def field was read:**
    - The volley pays `part.cost` and resets `cooldown_left = part.cooldown`.
    - `_continuous` pays `part.rate * delta`.
    - `volley_cost` sums `part.cost`.
    - `ChEnemyAI.should_press` sums `part.cost`, and `should_hold` sums `part.rate`.
    - `drive_cost()` sums the wheels' `rate`.
  - `ChPartRuntime.tick_rate()` keeps its rule. `ctx` passes it through `stat(part, STAT_TICK_RATE, part.tick_rate())` for both cooldowns and continuous ticks (R26 hooks it here).
  - `ChPartRuntime.bonus_damage: int = 0`.
    - `deal_hit` adds a hit key `"bonus": source.bonus_damage`, which modifiers may raise.
    - The applied damage is `(hit.damage + hit.bonus) * hit.multiplier`.
  - **Proximity through one helper:**
    - `ChBattleContext.range_of(part) -> float` is `stat(part, STAT_RANGE, def.proximity_range)`.
    - `ChBattleContext.near(a, b) -> bool` is true when the two are same side, unbroken and not benched, and either their distance ≤ `range_of(a)` or some live behaviour's `links(a, b)` answers true. The base `ChBehaviour.links` returns false.
    - `deal_hit`'s proximity loop and `ChEventBus._in_range` both call `near`. The bus holds a `near: Callable` that `ctx` sets.
  - **Scopes on `ChBehaviour`**, all answering unbroken, non-benched own parts other than `part`:
    - `_neighbours(ctx, tag := "") -> Array[ChPartRuntime]`
    - `_front_part(ctx, tag := "") -> ChPartRuntime` is the nearest neighbour with `(other.anchor - anchor).dot(facing()) > 0`.
    - `_back_part(ctx, tag := "") -> ChPartRuntime` is the same with `< 0`.
    - `_mounted() -> Array[ChPartRuntime]` is `part.children`, unbroken.
    - `_own_parts(ctx, tag := "")`
    - `_enemy_parts(ctx, tag := "")`
    - `_hit_enemy_body(ctx, damage: int)`
  - `ChEvent.BATTLE_STARTED` is emitted once by the first `tick`, before anything else, with no source.
  - `ChChariotRuntime.driving: bool` is set from `energy.tick`'s `drove` each tick.
  - `ChBattleContext.pay_trigger(part, cost: int) -> bool` is for triggered passives. It pays `stat(part, STAT_ENERGY_COST, cost)` or answers false (spec Stage 3).
  - Constants: `STAT_ENERGY_COST`, `STAT_ENERGY_PER_SECOND`, `STAT_COOLDOWN`, `STAT_TICK_RATE`, `STAT_RANGE`.

### Task 4: Sim and weapon helpers

**Files:**
- Modify: `game/battle/ch_battle_context.gd`, `game/battle/ch_sparks.gd`, `game/battle/behaviours/ch_behaviour.gd`, `game/battle/ch_projectile.gd`, `game/battle/ch_event.gd`
- Test: new `tests/battle/test_part_helpers.gd`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - **`ChBattleContext.spawn(source: ChPartRuntime, material: StringName, at: Vector2, cells: int) -> int`**
    - The count becomes `roundi(stat(source, &"spawn_" + material, cells))`.
    - It writes that many cells into air (or gas) cells nearest `at`, in a spiral outward. It never overwrites solids, parts or concrete.
    - It emits `ChEvent.CELLS_SPAWNED {material, count, at}` from `source`, and answers the count written.
    - For `gunpowder` and `tnt`, it also calls `own_charge` over the written cells' rect.
    - For `battery`, it calls `sparks.add_battery`.
  - **`ChBattleContext.throw_sparks(source, at: Vector2, hops := 1, first_only := false) -> Array`** writes `SPARK_CELLS := 6` spark cells about `at`, then calls `ChSparks.discharge(self, seeds, true, hops, first_only)`. It answers the holders shocked.
  - **`ChSparks.discharge(ctx, seeds, shock := true, hops := 1, first_only := false)`**
    - `hops` passes through to `give_status`.
    - `first_only` shocks only the first holder reached, in BFS order (U19).
    - The give is made with cause `&"sparks"`.
  - **`ChBattleContext.dig(at: Vector2i, radius: int, hardness: int) -> int`** removes cells of hardness ≤ `hardness` in the disc, never concrete, and answers the cells removed. It counts non-air in the disc's rect before and after `world.explode(at, radius, 0.0, hardness)`, and emits `CELLS_DUG {count}` when the count is above 0.
  - **`ChBattleContext.push(chariot, impulse: Vector2)`** is `chariot.chassis.apply_impulse(impulse)`, guarded for a freed chassis.
  - **`ChBattleContext.blow(rect: Rect2i, velocity: Vector2i) -> int`** wraps `world.blow`.
    - Ruling: wind moves grid cells only. Fire particles still in flight are not pushed (the Stage 2 carry-over, decided here).
    - `# ponytail:` mark it with the upgrade path: a particle push in `ChFx`'s pool.
  - **Weapon helpers on `ChBehaviour`:**
    - `_shoot(ctx, speed: float, gravity: float, on_impact: Callable) -> ChProjectile` fires from the part's nose along `facing()`. `on_impact` gets the raycast dict. The projectile frees itself after `LIFE`, so a target that is gone just ends the flight (Review Focus 2).
    - `_lob(ctx, speed, on_impact)` is `_shoot` with `LOB_GRAVITY := 60.0`, angled 45° up along facing.
    - `_beam_target(ctx) -> Object` is `_nearest_enemy_body` over `_reach_box()`.
    - `_spray_box(ctx) -> Rect2` is `_reach_box()`, for cell spawning along a beam.
  - `ChBattleContext.force_fire(part, by: ChPartRuntime)` is the spec's forced fire. It lands in Task 16, not here.
