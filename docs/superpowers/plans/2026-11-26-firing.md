# The Firing Rule (Stage 3 of All Parts) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Keep the one-button rule and add holding. A press fires every ready cooldown part, as today. Holding runs every continuous (melee and beam) part and pays it per second. Wheels pay per second while driving. Weapons get their own reach field, and melee reach is proven by a contact test across every pair of bodies.

**Architecture:**
- `ChPartDef` gains three fields:
  - `mode` (`cooldown`, `melee`, `beam`, `passive` or `wheel`)
  - `energy_per_second`
  - `reach` (cells from the part's edge)
- `ChBattleContext.tick` takes a held state per side. Continuous parts run in a new step, after the drive and the volley.
- A per-part tick timer drives melee and beam seconds. The behaviour answers through one new hook, `continuous_tick`.
- `ChBehaviour` gains a shared `_strike` helper for "hit the nearest enemy body in my reach box".
- `ChEnemyAI` gains `should_hold`. `ChBattle` and the balance runner feed the held state the same way they feed presses.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4, GDScript, the `noita_engine` PixelWorld, the repo's headless test runner, `tools/balance`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-all-parts-design.md`. The binding parts are Stage 3, "Decided by the user", "Constraints", and the anti-goals. The firing rules come from `docs/design-doc/parts-list.md` §1.2 and `docs/design-doc/core-mechanics.md` §3.2–§3.3.

## Global Constraints

- **Tests.**
  - Run one file's tests: `godot --headless --path . -s res://tests/run_tests.gd -- <file-name-filter>`.
  - The whole suite (no filter) must end `N passed, 0 failed` at the end of every task.
  - Run `godot --headless --path . --import` once first, and again after adding a `.tres` or a `class_name`.
  - Smoke: `bash tools/balance/run.sh --suite smoke` must finish ok.
  - Known noise to ignore: engine RID-leak warnings at exit, `ERROR: Condition "!props.has(pinfo.name)"` on import, and the wall-clock flake `test_tower_drives_upright_on_slow_frames`.
- **Determinism.** Fights stay deterministic for a seed. Nothing here draws a random number.
- **Same rules both sides.** The player and the enemy run on the same rules. Each side's input is a drive direction, a press edge and a held state. The enemy's come from `ChEnemyAI`, and nothing else differs.
- **One damage path.** Every damage goes through `ChBattleContext.deal_hit`, and every announcement goes through `ctx.bus`.
- **Firing modes.**
  - `&"cooldown"`: fires on a press when ready, and pays `energy_cost` per firing.
  - `&"melee"` and `&"beam"`: continuous. They run while held, and pay `energy_per_second` per second.
  - `&"wheel"`: pays `energy_per_second` while the chariot drives.
  - `&"passive"`: neither.
- **Continuous ticks are one second each,** scaled by the part's statuses: Speedup ×2 rate, Slow ×0.5, Freeze stops the part entirely.
  - A melee tick is a hit and a firing (`PART_FIRED`).
  - A beam tick is a hit only. A beam's `PART_FIRED` comes once, when it switches on. That is the first running tick after it wasn't running: after a release, after a tick it couldn't be paid, or at the start of the fight.
- **Ordering inside `_tick_chariot`:**
  1. cooldowns
  2. `energy.tick` (regen, then the drive at the wheels' summed cost)
  3. the volley, on a press
  4. **continuous parts, if held**
  5. `process`
  6. `drive()`
- **The melee test is the authority on reach, not a number.** Melee reach starts at 24 cells from the part's edge. It goes up only if the Task 4 contact test needs it.

## Left to Stage 4

The spec's Stage 3 list also rules on two things that only parts use. They land with the first part that needs them, as small helpers on `ChBattleContext`:
- **Triggered passives with an energy cost** (C10 Bayonet, C15 First Aid Kit, U77 Harpoon) pay when they trigger, and skip that trigger if the pool can't cover it.
- **A forced fire** (R15, R33, R27) costs no energy, doesn't touch the forced part's cooldown, and still counts as firing.

Also for Stage 4: U60 Priority Relay and R28 Blood Cell change the volley's order and payment. Their hooks go into the volley code Task 2 leaves in place.

## Review Focus

1. **Tapping the button to beat the tick rate.** Rapidly holding and releasing must never land melee ticks faster than one per second of running. The timer counts down only while running and is never reset by a release (Task 2 tests it).
2. **A held button with an empty pool.** A continuous part that can't be paid simply doesn't run that tick. Nothing goes negative, the drive still came first, and the beam fires `PART_FIRED` again when it next runs (Task 2 tests it).
3. **A frozen continuous part.** It doesn't run, doesn't pay and doesn't count down, so its timer is unchanged across the freeze (Task 2 tests it).
4. **A wheel breaks mid-drive.** The drive cost falls to the unbroken wheels' sum that same tick, and a chariot with no unbroken wheels pays nothing to "drive" (Task 1 tests it).
5. **Every body pair meets in melee.** With the front-slot melee part, the attacker lands a tick after the hulls touch, for all nine attacker/defender pairs (Task 4 tests it).

### Task 2: Holding runs continuous parts

**Files:**
- Modify: `game/battle/ch_battle_context.gd` (the `hold_input` parameter, the continuous step, `PART_SKIPPED`, `continuous_cost`)
- Modify: `game/battle/ch_part_runtime.gd` (`tick_left`, `running`, `can_run()`)
- Modify: `game/battle/behaviours/ch_behaviour.gd` (`continuous_tick` hook, `_strike` helper)
- Modify: `game/battle/ch_event.gd` (`PART_SKIPPED`)
- Modify: `game/battle/ch_battle.gd` (`_show_ready` also lights continuous parts)
- Create: `tests/battle/fixtures/ch_fixture_melee.gd` and `tests/battle/fixtures/ch_fixture_beam.gd`. Both are behaviours whose `continuous_tick` returns `_strike(ctx)`.
- Test: new `tests/battle/test_continuous.gd`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1's `mode`, `energy_per_second`, `reach` and `is_continuous()`.
- Produces:
  - `ChBattleContext.tick(delta, drive_input, press_input := [], hold_input := []) -> Array[int]`. A side with no `hold_input` entry reads not held. The return value is unchanged: the parts fired by the volley per side.
  - `ChPartRuntime`:
    - `tick_left: float = 0.0` is the seconds until its next continuous tick.
    - `running: bool = false` is whether it ran on the last tick.
    - `can_run() -> bool` is true when it's continuous, has a def, isn't broken and isn't frozen.
  - The continuous step runs when the side is held.
    - Order: every `can_run()` part, dearest `energy_per_second` first, ties broken by mount order. That's the volley's rule.
    - Each part tries `energy.try_pay(def.energy_per_second * delta)`.
    - **Paid:**
      - If `running` was false and the mode is beam, emit `PART_FIRED`.
      - Set `running = true`.
      - Count down `tick_left -= delta * rate`. The rate is 2 under Speedup, 0.5 under Slow, otherwise 1.
      - When `tick_left <= 0`, call `behaviour.continuous_tick(ctx)`. If it answers true (it landed), set `tick_left = 1.0`, and for melee emit `PART_FIRED`. If it answers false, `tick_left` stays at 0 and it tries again next tick.
    - **Not paid, or not held:** set `running = false`. `tick_left` is left as it is.
  - `ChBehaviour`:
    - `continuous_tick(ctx) -> bool` is the hook. The base answers false.
    - `_strike(ctx) -> bool` finds the nearest enemy body in `_reach_box()`. If there is one, it calls `ctx.deal_hit(part, target, part.def.damage)` and answers true.
  - `ChEvent.PART_SKIPPED` is emitted in the volley for each ready part that `try_pay` refused. The source is the part and the data is `{"cost": energy_cost}`. This is the spec's "a part is skipped in a volley" trigger.
  - `ChBattleContext.continuous_cost(chariot) -> float` is the summed `energy_per_second` of its `can_run()` parts. It's for the HUD and the AI.
  - `ChBattle._show_ready` also lights a part's outline when it is `can_run()` and the pool holds at least its `energy_per_second * (1.0 / 60.0)`. If continuous parts have no outline built, the builder builds one for them the way it does for cooldown parts.

- Test: `tests/battle/test_enemy_ai.gd`, `tests/battle/test_battle.gd`, and the balance tests under `tests/balance/` if they pin the driver set

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 2's `can_run()` and `continuous_cost`, and the `hold_input` of `tick`.
- Produces:
  - `ChEnemyAI.HOLD_SHARE := 0.5`.
  - `ChEnemyAI.should_hold(chariot, target_x, target_half) -> bool` is true when two things hold:
    - Some `can_run()` part has the target in reach, measured as `should_press` measures a reach part: `absf(target_x - part.anchor_world().x) <= def.reach + target_half`.
    - The pool holds at least `HOLD_SHARE` × the summed `energy_per_second` of those in-reach parts.
  - `ChBattle.holders: Array` follows the same contract as `pressers`: `func(battle, side) -> bool` is the held state this tick.
    - The defaults are `_player_hold()` (`Input.is_action_pressed(&"fire")`) for side 0 and `ai_hold(1)` for side 1.
    - `ai_hold(side)` mirrors `ai_press`.
  - Balance runner: each side's hold policy follows its press policy, so the scenario schema doesn't change. `"ai"` → `battle.ai_hold(side)`, `"mash"` → true, `"never"` → false. Ruling: no separate hold key until a suite needs one.
