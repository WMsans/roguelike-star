# Statuses (Stage 1 of All Parts) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the burning flag, the 0..1 freeze meter and the chariot-wide burn with the 9 stacking statuses of `elements-list.md` §1–§2 on every part and on the body, fed by the sand sim, readable on screen.

**Architecture:**
- `ChStatuses` is a pure stack holder: gain with cancel and doubling, decay, Block absorption, and material → gain rates.
- Every `ChPartRuntime` and every `ChChariotRuntime` (the body) owns one.
- `ChBattleContext.give_status` is the single entry point that applies a gain, emits events, puffs steam and jumps Shock.
- A once-per-second status clock in `ChBattleContext.tick` samples materials, deals status damage, acts on cells and decays.
- The 12 old parts keep working on the new rules until Stage 4 retires them.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4, GDScript, the `noita_engine` PixelWorld, the repo's headless test runner.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-all-parts-design.md` (Stage 1, plus "Decided by the user"). Rules and numbers come from `docs/design-doc/elements-list.md` §1, §2, §5 and §6.

## Global Constraints

- Run one file's tests: `godot --headless --path . -s res://tests/run_tests.gd -- <file-name-filter>`.
- Run the whole suite: `godot --headless --path . -s res://tests/run_tests.gd`.
  - It must end `N passed, 0 failed` at the end of every task.
  - Run `godot --headless --path . --import` once first.
- Fights stay deterministic for a seed. Nothing here draws a random number; cell scans are row-major from the rect's top-left.
- Stacks are `int`. A status never goes below 0.
- Status names are these `StringName`s, in this order everywhere a list is shown: `flame, freeze, block, acid, wet, oil, shock, speedup, slow`.
- Parts never give Flame, Freeze, Acid, Wet, Oil or Shock directly (parts-list §1.3). Two exceptions until Stage 4 retires them: the old Freeze Charge and Mace, which go through `hit["freeze"]` and `hit["thaw"]`.
- Status damage (Flame, Acid, Shock) wears integrity on a part and HP on the body. It never does both.
- Weapon hits lower HP and integrity together, after Block.

## Calls this plan makes (not pinned by the spec)

- **Block and status damage:** Block absorbs weapon hits and Acid. Flame and Shock go straight past Block. Reason: `elements-list.md` singles out Acid as "hitting Block first".
- **The fire handicap** (`rules.fire_mult`) scales the Flame damage that side-1 holders take, in place of the old burn tick.
- **Lava** no longer wears integrity directly. It gives +6 Flame per second, and Flame does the wearing.
- **Shock's effect on cells** (sparks into water and steel) waits for Stage 2's spark material.
- **What each status does to touching cells, per status second:**
  - Flame throws 1 fire particle from the holder, and the sim's own fire rules do the igniting.
  - Freeze turns up to 4 water cells in the sample rect into ice.
  - Acid removes the first sand, wood, gunpowder or TNT cell in the sample rect.
  - Oil, every second status second, puts 1 oil cell in the first air cell under the holder's box.
- **The steam puff** when Flame and Wet cancel is 4 `steam` particles.

## Review Focus

1. **A part broken by status damage mid-second.** Its children cascade through `break_part`, and a broken part is not damaged, decayed or sampled again that second. Owner: Task 4 (`test_status_break_mid_second`).
2. **A long frame.** A `tick(2.5, …)` (the balance runner's coarse steps, a hitch) runs the status second twice and keeps 0.5 s on the clock, rather than once or not at all. Owner: Task 4 (`test_long_tick_runs_two_status_seconds`).
3. **A dead chariot.** Once HP is 0, no further status damage takes it below 0, and the status second neither crashes nor emits for it. Owner: Task 4 (`test_dead_chariot_takes_no_status_second`).
4. **A packed cluster of Wet parts.** Shock jumps at most two hops, never back to the part it came from, and stays under the bus cap without a delivery-cap warning. Owner: Task 3 (`test_shock_on_wet_cluster_stops_after_two_hops`).
5. **A status given to a broken part, a null target or 0 stacks.** It adds nothing and emits nothing. Owner: Task 3 (`test_give_status_ignores_broken_null_and_zero`).

### Task 1: `ChStatuses`, the stack holder

**Files:**

**Interfaces:**
- Produces `class_name ChStatuses extends RefCounted`:
  - `const FLAME := &"flame"`, plus `FREEZE`, `BLOCK`, `ACID`, `WET`, `OIL`, `SHOCK`, `SPEEDUP` and `SLOW` the same way.
  - `const ALL: Array[StringName]`: the 9, in Global Constraints order.
  - `const DECAYS`: `[FLAME, FREEZE, ACID, SHOCK, SPEEDUP, SLOW]`.
  - `const CANCELS`: `{FLAME: [WET, FREEZE], FREEZE: [FLAME], WET: [FLAME, ACID], ACID: [WET], SPEEDUP: [SLOW], SLOW: [SPEEDUP]}`. A gain cancels these in list order.
  - `const GAIN_RATES`: `{&"fire": [FLAME, 2], &"lava": [FLAME, 6], &"frost": [FREEZE, 3], &"ice": [FREEZE, 1], &"water": [WET, 3], &"steam": [WET, 1], &"oil": [OIL, 3], &"acid": [ACID, 3], &"smoke": [SLOW, 1]}`.
  - `func count(status: StringName) -> int`
  - `func gain(status: StringName, amount: int) -> Dictionary` returns `{"added": int, "cancelled": Array[[StringName, int]], "doubled": int}`. The order:
    1. If `status == WET` and Oil > 0, it adds nothing.
    2. Otherwise the gain cancels per `CANCELS`, one for one.
    3. On the remainder: Flame with Oil, or Freeze with Wet, gains extra equal to `min(remainder, that stack)` and uses that stack up.
    4. The result is added.
  - `func clear(status: StringName) -> int`: stacks removed.
  - `func decay() -> Array[StringName]`: −1 on each status in `DECAYS` above 0. Returns those that reached 0.
  - `func absorb(damage: int) -> int`: Block used up against `damage`, returned.
  - `func any() -> bool`
  - `static func material_gains(counts: Dictionary, area: int) -> Dictionary`: status → stacks.
    - Each material present (count > 0) gives its rate, and doubles when `count * 2 > area`.
    - Materials mapping to the same status add up.
    - Unknown materials give nothing.

**What `_status_second` does,** for each chariot with `hp > 0`, over a snapshot of `[chariot] + unbroken parts`:
1. **Gain.** Read `world.count_materials(holder.sample_rect())`, with area = rect width × height. For each `(status, n)` in `ChStatuses.material_gains`, call `give_status`.
2. **Damage.** Steps 2–4 skip any part that broke earlier in this pass. Damage is:
   - Flame × (`rules.fire_mult` if the holder's side is 1, else 1), rounded
   - plus Acid minus `statuses.absorb(Acid)`
   - plus Shock

   A part loses that much integrity, and `break_part` runs at 0. The body loses that much HP, clamped at 0.
3. **Cells.** Apply the per-status cell effects from "Calls this plan makes". Skip this when `world == null`.
4. **Decay.** Call `statuses.decay()` and emit `STATUS_LOST` for each status returned.

**`tick` order:**
1. Hazards.
2. `status_clock += delta`, then `while status_clock >= STATUS_SECOND`: subtract it and call `_status_second()`.
3. Per chariot, the existing pass without `apply_sample` and without the burn block.

**Rates:**
- `tick_cooldown(delta)` pauses on `is_frozen()`, and otherwise counts `delta × (2.0 if Speedup > 0, 0.5 if Slow > 0, else 1.0)`.
- `energy.tick` gets `regen_mult`: 0 if the body has Freeze, 2 if Speedup, 0.5 if Slow, else 1.

**`deal_hit`:**
- Pass `target_part.statuses.count(BLOCK)`, or the body's Block for a hull hit, into `ChDamage`.
- Write the returned block back as `statuses.stacks[BLOCK]`.
- `hit["freeze"]` is now an `int` of stacks, defaulting to 0, given with `give_status(freeze_part, FREEZE, n)`.
- `hit["thaw"]` calls `statuses.clear(FREEZE)` and emits `STATUS_LOST {status: freeze}` if anything was removed.

**Ports:**
- **Freeze Charge:** `FREEZE_PER_HIT := 3`.
- **Mace:** Shatter reads `target.is_frozen()` and no longer emits its own thaw event.
- **Rocket and Ember Battery:** listen for "caught fire" as defined above.
- **Feedback:** the `caught_fire`, `frozen` and `thawed` profiles play on those same conditions.
- **Fight stats:** keep their `caught_fire` and `frozen` counters and output keys, fed by those conditions, so `tools/balance/analysis/*.py` is unchanged.
- **`_draw_markers`:** keeps only the broken X. Task 5 draws statuses.
