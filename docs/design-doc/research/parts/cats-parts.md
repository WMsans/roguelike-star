# CATS: Crash Arena Turbo Stars, All Parts

Every machine part documented on the CATS fan wiki, pulled on 2026-11-03.

Source: https://catsthegame.fandom.com (pages: Body, Weapons, Gadgets, Wheels, Championship Machines, Ultimate Machines, Megabuild, Skills, Toolboxes, Co-Pilot). The fandom wiki is small and partly incomplete: per-star numeric stats for Championship parts aren't recorded there, and some Ultimate parts lack text. Gaps are left blank, not guessed.

## How machines work (summary)

- A machine = **Body** (chassis) + parts in its slots. Bodies have hexagonal **weapon** slots (1-3), round **wheel** slots (2-3, along the bottom) and square **gadget** slots (0+). Slot positions are random per body.
- **Health** comes from body + wheels + gadgets. **Power** (energy) is a body budget; every weapon and gadget costs power.
- Battles are fully automatic physics fights: you build, then watch. Placement, weight and balance decide whether you flip.
- **Championship** parts come in 5 tiers × 5 stars: Wood (yellow), Metal (blue), Military (green), Gold, Carbon (red). Max level by stars: 1★=6, 2★=11, 3★=16, 4★=21, 5★=26. A 1★ of a higher tier beats a 5★ of the lower tier. Fusing parts gives XP/levels.
- **Magic bonus** (Super parts 10-60%, Legendary 100%): % Attack or Health to a specific part type, or universal (to all bodies/weapons/wheels/gadgets, or when equipped).
- **Ultimate League** is a separate parts set with 6 rarities: Standard R1, Polished R2, Refined R3, Superior R4, Outstanding R5, Extraordinary R6. R5+ have a universal bonus; R6 parts have a **sponsor** (Gluttony Ltd, Naturalis Inc, Mecha Corp). 3+ parts of one sponsor = +10% Attack and HP, +5% per extra part. Each upgrade level = +20% stats (≈ +8% past level 16).

### Weapon mechanics notes

- Attack shown is damage per second. Laser fires every 2.5 s, so one shot = Attack × 2.5 (toolbox bonus added after the multiplier).
- Rocket and Double Rocket fire once per second; Double Rocket fires forward and back.
- Minigun spins up, then fires 5 shots/s.
- Shotgun fires 7 pellets about half the arena's range and knocks both cars back.
- Boomerang hits on the way out and back (2× if both connect). Death Lamp and Boomerang auto-aim.
- Melee reach, shortest to longest: Chainsaw (most damage) < Drill / Blade < Stinger (least damage). The Blade spins, so it still hits when the machine is flipped.
- Gadget firing order: Fishnet → Harpoon → Tranquilizer → Lifter with Booster/Backpedal. First Aid Kit (below 50% HP), Repulse, Forklift are situational.
